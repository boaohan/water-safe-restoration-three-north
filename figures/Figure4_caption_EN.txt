Figure 4. Pareto optimization of water-safe vegetation structures.

(a) Sand-fixation benefit versus water-pressure scores and (b) ecological-stability versus GDE-risk scores. Colored circles show all 480 exported Pareto records from 25 optimized analytical units, with a shared continuous reversed viridis scale for target leaf area index (LAI), softened to a pale ivory-yellow at low values and transitioning through teal to dark blue-purple at high values. Black open diamonds identify the 25 selected compromises supplied in the revised selection file. These are two-dimensional projections of the per-unit four-objective Pareto sets; overlapping records retain their original coordinates, and compromises were not reselected from either projection.

(c) Numbers of analytical units assigned to the selected vegetation structures: low-tree mixed, 22 (88%); shrub–herbaceous, 3 (12%). Percentages refer to the 25 optimized analytical units, not to proportions of the study-area surface.

(d) Pareto min–max ranges of tree, shrub, and herbaceous proportions, ordered by ascending ClassCode. Colored horizontal segments with end caps show the minima and maxima among the exported Pareto solutions; filled circles with thin black borders indicate the supplied selected compromises. A fixed range is shown as a single point. Segments are not 95% confidence intervals and do not imply that every intermediate value corresponds to a feasible solution. The proportions are conditional on vegetated area, with q_tree + q_shrub + q_herb = 1. Thus, q_tree = 0.2 denotes 20% of vegetated area; its whole-surface fraction is V_target × q_tree. Only the 25 optimized units are included; the 33 direct-management units are excluded.
